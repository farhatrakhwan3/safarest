About Safarest

Safarest is a creative workspace built exclusively for civil engineers. From structural estimation and CAD drafting to QA/QC lab calculations, surveying tools, and PDF utilities — everything an engineer needs lives in one place, right in the browser.

What you'll find here

  • Estimation Tools — Footings, beams, columns, slabs & retaining walls.
  • CAD & Sheet Workspace — AutoCAD-style drawing with an integrated spreadsheet.
  • QA/QC Lab Tools — Moisture, sieve analysis, cube tests, silt & bulkage.
  • Surveying Utilities — Simpson's rule & area calculations.
  • Civil Aptitude — Unit weight, area & volume formulas.
  • PDF Editor — Merge, split, rotate, crop, lock & unlock documents.

Dedicated to all Civil Engineers.
